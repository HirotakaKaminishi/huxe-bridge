# Kiro achieves FedRAMP High and DoD IL-4/5 authorization in AWS GovCloud (US)

<p>Kiro is now FedRAMP High and Department of Defense Cloud Computing Security Requirements Guide (DoD CC SRG) Impact Level (IL) 4 and 5 authorized in the AWS GovCloud (US) Regions.<br /> <br /> Federal agencies, public sector organizations, and other enterprises with FedRAMP High and DoD CC SRG IL-4/5 compliance requirements can now use Kiro as their agentic engineering partner with confidence that it meets the security and compliance standards required for sensitive workloads.<br /> <br /> Kiro is an agentic AI with an integrated development environment (IDE) and command-line interface (CLI) that helps you build applications from prototype to production with spec-driven development. From simple to complex tasks, Kiro works alongside you to turn prompts into detailed specs, then into working code, docs, and tests — so what you build is exactly what you want and ready to share with your team. With native Model Context Protocol (MCP) support, Kiro connects to documentation, databases, APIs, and other enterprise resources, providing capability for mission-critical development workflows.<br /> <br /> For more details about Kiro in AWS GovCloud (US), visit the <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/govcloud-us/latest/UserGuide/govcloud-kiro.html">GovCloud documentation</a> or contact your AWS account team for more information. To learn more about Kiro, visit the <a href="https://kiro.dev/">Kiro product page</a>.</p>
