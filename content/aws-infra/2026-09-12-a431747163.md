# Amazon EC2 X2idn instances are now available in Asia Pacific (Hong Kong)

<p>Memory-optimized Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (Amazon EC2) X2idn instances are now available in Asia Pacific (Hong Kong) Region. These instances, powered by 3rd generation Intel Xeon Scalable Processors and built with AWS Nitro System, are designed for memory-intensive workloads and deliver improvements in performance compared to previous generation X1 instances. These instances are SAP-certified for running Business Suite on HANA, SAP S/4HANA, Data Mart Solutions on HANA, Business Warehouse on HANA, SAP BW/4HANA, and SAP NetWeaver workloads on any database.</p>
