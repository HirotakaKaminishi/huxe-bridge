# AWS Quick now supports plugins to bundle agents, skills, connectors and flows

<p>AWS Quick now supports plugins, agents, skills, connectors, and flows built for a specific task, role, or use case. Most work draws on several of these at once: connectors to reach your systems, skills for how your team works, agents to reason through tasks, and flows to orchestrate each process. Plugins package these capabilities together, so users can install everything a job needs in one step.</p> 
<p>To get started, users can browse and install official plugins from AWS, such as the Calendar and Meetings Assistant, which helps users plan time away and turn meeting notes into action items, or the Sales Development plugin, which takes a prospect from lead research to outreach and a proposal. More than 100 skills and plugins cover everyday productivity, roles such as finance, marketing and legal, industries, and popular business applications.</p> 
<p>Builders can also create their own custom plugins for their team, department, or organization. Just describe the job in chat, and Quick assembles the plugin. Then share it for teammates to discover.</p> 
<p>This is available in all Regions where AWS Quick is available.</p> 
<p>To learn more, see the <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/what-is.html">AWS Quick User Guide</a>. To get started with Quick, visit <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/quick/">AWS Quick</a>.</p>
