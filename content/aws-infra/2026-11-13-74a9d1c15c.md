# AWS Marketplace now supports VAT credit notes for deemed supply refunds

<p>AWS Marketplace sellers can now submit Value Added Tax (VAT) credit notes for refunded deemed supply transactions through a self-service experience in AWS Partner Central. Previously, VAT was disbursed to sellers for eligible transactions, and when a buyer was later refunded, sellers had to contact AWS to submit the corresponding credit note. This launch simplifies post-refund VAT documentation for sellers.</p>
<p>Sellers can view eligible refund line items in the VAT on deemed supply section, select the refunds covered by a credit note, upload a PDF or the applicable electronic invoice format, and track the submission status. Sellers can consolidate multiple eligible refunds into a single credit note where applicable and review the associated financial adjustment in the Seller Insights reporting.</p>
<p>These capabilities are available for eligible AWS Marketplace transactions, including Channel Partner Private Offer (CPPO) transactions, in supported EU member states, Norway, and the United Kingdom. Country-specific invoicing and electronic-invoicing requirements continue to apply.</p>
<p>To get started, sign in to AWS Partner Central and go to Settings → Tax information → VAT on deemed supply. To learn more, visit the <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/vat-on-deemed-supply.html" target="_blank">AWS Marketplace Seller Guide</a> or <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/vat-deemed-supply-faq.html" target="_blank">Deemed Supply FAQs</a>.</p>
