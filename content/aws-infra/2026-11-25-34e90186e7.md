# Amazon Bedrock adds bedrock-runtime endpoint support for OpenAI Daybreak Red in AWS GovCloud (US)

<p>Eligible customers can now access OpenAI’s specialized cybersecurity models Daybreak Red: GPT-5.6 Cyber through the bedrock-runtime endpoint on Amazon Bedrock in AWS GovCloud (US).<br /> <br /> Daybreak Red is designed for advanced, authorized tasks such as vulnerability research, exploit reproduction, and mitigation development. For these tasks, a lower refusal threshold, matched by stronger identity verification, monitoring, and access controls, improves the speed and depth of an investigation. Your inference data is not used for model training, and you are not required to opt in to sharing your data with OpenAI.<br /> <br /> On the bedrock-runtime endpoint, the model is available through the US Gov Geo cross-Region inference (CRIS) profile. Access to the model requires enrollment in <a href="https://openai.com/form/enterprise-trusted-access-for-cyber/">Daybreak access</a> from OpenAI. To enroll, contact OpenAI or reach out to your AWS account team for guidance on eligibility. Once approved, work with your account team to request access on AWS. For regional availability through the bedrock-mantle endpoint and more information, view the<a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-card-openai-gpt-56-cyber.html"> documentation.</a></p>
