# Amazon Aurora now supports post-quantum TLS key exchange

<p>Starting today, <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/rds/aurora/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL-Compatible Edition</a> introduces support for post-quantum TLS (PQ-TLS) key exchange, providing you with post-quantum cryptography options for encrypting your data in transit.</p> 
<p>Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL versions 18 and higher support modification of the ssl_groups parameter. You can <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/AuroraUserGuide/AuroraPostgreSQL.Security.html#PostgreSQL.Concepts.General.SSL.Groups" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">select cryptographic groups</a> from the Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL allow list to align with your organization's security standards.</p> 
<p>Amazon Aurora is designed for high performance and availability at global scale with full PostgreSQL compatibility. It provides scale-to-zero serverless compute, Aurora Global Database for multi-Region resilience, Aurora I/O-Optimized for improved price performance on I/O-intensive workloads, and built-in security and continuous backups. To get started, take a look at our <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/AuroraUserGuide/CHAP_GettingStartedAurora.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">getting started page</a>.</p>
