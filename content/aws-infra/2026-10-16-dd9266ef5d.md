# AWS End User Messaging adds a guided wizard for RCS registration videos

<p>Today, AWS End User Messaging adds a wizard that orchestrates the entire video capture for your RCS agent registration. RCS registrations in multiple countries, including the United States, require a screen recording that demonstrates your messaging experience. To record it, you have to send a specific sequence of messages to a test device, and until now you scripted those messages yourself, which easily introduced errors and made a mismatched or incomplete video one of the hardest parts of registering for RCS.
 <br />
 <br />
 The wizard now drives each step of the capture for you, sending the right messages in the right order, so you record a compliant video the first time and get your agent approved sooner. The wizard is on all RCS registration forms that require a video submission.
 <br />
 <br />
 To learn more and get started, see the AWS End User Messaging <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/end-user-messaging/latest/userguide/nx-overview.html">User Guide.</a></p>
