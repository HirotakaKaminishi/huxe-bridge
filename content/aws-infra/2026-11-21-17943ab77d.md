# AWS Quick launches a new web experience with updated browser extension and Microsoft 365 add-ins

<p>The AWS Quick web app now brings the Quick desktop experience to your browser. Create, share, and use the same agents and skills as on desktop, with the same connectors. Start each day with a daily briefing and a prioritized feed of the email and messages that need your attention. Quick keeps your conversations, agents, and files synchronized across web, desktop, and mobile, so work you start on one surface carries over to the others. Your dashboards, topics, and spaces work as before.</p> 
<p>Additionally, agents you start or schedule from the web keep running after you close your browser. Schedule an agent in the morning, answer its questions from the mobile app between meetings, and review the results on your desktop. Mission Control shows what your agents have finished and where they need your input. The Quick browser extension and Microsoft 365 add-ins for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook are also updated.</p> 
<p>These updates are available in the following AWS Regions: US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (Ireland), Europe (London), Asia Pacific (Sydney), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), and US East (N. Virginia).</p> 
<p>Existing Quick users can sign in to the web app to get started, or download the desktop app <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/quick/download/">here</a>. The Quick mobile app is available for free from the <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/amazon-quick/id1148226615">Apple App Store</a> and <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amazonaws.quicksight.mobile&hl=en_US">Google Play Store</a>. To learn more, see the <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/">AWS Quick User Guide</a>.</p>
