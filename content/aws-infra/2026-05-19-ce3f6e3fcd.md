# Amazon EVS enables support for 32 hosts per environment

<p>Today, we are announcing that Amazon Elastic VMware Service (Amazon EVS) now supports up to 32 ESXi hosts per environment, double the previous limit of 16 hosts.</p> 
<p>Amazon EVS gives you flexibility in how you configure VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) domains and clusters within an environment. You can put all your hosts into a single large cluster, spread them across several smaller clusters, or any combination that fits your needs. With this release, you can now submit a service quota increase to scale up to a total of 32 hosts and reduce the operational overhead of managing multiple environments.</p> 
<p>This latest release is available in all regions where Amazon EVS is offered.</p> 
<p>For more details on the steps and procedure, visit the Amazon EVS <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/evs/">product detail page</a> and <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/evs/latest/userguide/what-is-evs.html">user guide.</a></p>
